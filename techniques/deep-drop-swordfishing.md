# Deep-Drop Swordfishing (daytime)

Execution for daytime broadbill: a dead bait presented **in or just below the
[deep scattering layer](../conditions/deep-scattering-layer.md)** on deep contour
structure, on a slow drift, with a breakaway lead. Where/when this applies and
how to pick the spot is the router: [swordfish](../species/swordfish.md). Numbers
here are **SoCal**; see the East-coast contrast at the end.

## Reach for this when

- Daytime, deep contour break (150–350 fm) with the DSL present at ≥600 ft **and**
  chunky bait balls at 100–150+ fm on the meter.
- Fair weather: **wind <13 kt, drift ≤ ~2–2.5 kt** (a **drift sock** extends
  marginal days). >15 kt = no-go.

## Drift setup

- Set the drift **up-current of the fathom line** so the bait sweeps the
  structure as you drift down onto it. Preferred drift is **"down and in"** along
  the contour edge.
- Deploy **two baits** staggered in the column; budget **~7–9 minutes** to get
  both down and fishing.
- Baits ride the layer and **100–250 ft below** it — SoCal baits **average
  800–1,100 ft**, shallower when the layer is shallow (DSL at 850 → bait ~900).

## Rigging parameters (SoCal)

- **Lead:** breakaway **7–10.5 lb matched to the drift** — heavier when the
  drift is faster — on a light stick / rubber-band breakaway so it drops on the
  bite.
- **Leader:** heavy [bite-leader class](../rigging/bite-leaders.md) (200 lb+),
  run a **short leader** (swivel close to the rod tip so the fish is in gaff
  range at the swivel).
- **Bait:** dead squid, mackerel, or mullet-class, rigged to swim/track on the
  drop.

## Bite handling

- The best hookups come from **rods nobody touches** — let the fish wolf it
  (buoy-gear logic; Duane Diego Mellor / Rush Maltz). Resist grabbing the rod on
  the first taps.
- The breakaway lead drops on the hookset so you fight the fish clean.

## Common failures

- Dropping on a **bare DSL with no bait balls** — the bait *in* the layer is the
  trigger, not the layer alone.
- Too much drift (>2.5 kt) — presentation dies; deploy the sock or move.
- Touching the rod early and pulling the bait from a tentative fish.

## East-coast contrast (labeled — not SoCal doctrine)

East-coast/Gulf Stream deep-dropping runs **bottom-oriented (~1,800 ft)**,
**heavier 12–15 lb leads**, a faster Gulf Stream drift, and **long leaders** —
different enough that the numbers do not transfer. Use the SoCal parameters above
for the Bight.
