# Making Bait

"Making bait" is **catching your own live bait** before you go fishing, as
opposed to buying it. In SoCal the two paths are the **bait barge** (buy
sardines) and **making your own** (catch mackerel on sabikis, or net anchovy /
sardine). Once you have it, keep it alive per [bait tanks](bait-tanks.md) and
fish it per [fishing live bait](fishing-live-bait.md).

## Buy vs. make — Cameron's split

- **When buying:** sardines from the bait barge when they're available.
- **When making bait: it's always mackerel.** Mackerel are the bait he catches
  himself; sardines he buys.
- **Bait shortages (July 2026)** pushed him onto sabikis more often — both at
  **La Jolla** and the **Coronado Islands** — to make his own when the barge
  couldn't fill him.

## Sabiki work (mackerel)

A sabiki is a multi-hook bait-catching rig fished vertically. Cameron's mackerel
program:

- **Where:** the **Coronado Islands** (also La Jolla during shortages).
- **How deep:** drops the sabiki to **~100 ft**.
- **Sinker:** **8 oz** to get the string down and hold it.
- **Result:** has **loaded 5 twelve-inch mackerel on one drop** — a full string
  of quality bait in a single pass.

Twelve-inch mackerel are big, hearty baits — heartier than sardine (see
[fishing live bait](fishing-live-bait.md) for how bait choice plays against the
gamefish).

## Dedicated sabiki setup (building)

**Open item (attributed to Cameron, not doctrine):** he's building an
always-rigged, dedicated bait rod so a sabiki is ready to drop without re-tying:

- A **hollow sabiki rod that stores the rigged sabiki inside the blank** — the
  string tucks up into the rod so it travels rigged and tangle-free.
- Paired with a **levelwind line-counter reel** — the counter puts the string
  back at a known depth (e.g. ~100 ft) every drop.
- Intended as one always-rigged bait rod covering **SoCal and BOLA** (Bahía de
  los Ángeles).

## Cast net (considering)
