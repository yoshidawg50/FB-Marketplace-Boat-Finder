# Spreader Bar

A spreader bar is a rigid horizontal bar carrying a teaser school of hookless
squids or birds with one stinger lure trailing on the center line — it presents
as a small bait ball rather than a single lure, which is why it produces on
school-grade bluefin. It runs in the **80–100 lb top-shot class** and lives in
the **elevated (shotgun) holder** because the bar itself rides on the surface.
This note covers the **Half Fish Bait Co** bars, the ones Cameron fishes.

## Running specs

- **Troll speed:** 4–14 kt — **match the rest of the spread** (bars are the
  speed-agnostic member of the spread, so they follow whatever the other lures
  need).
- **Running depth:** the **bar rides out of the water** — it is a surface
  teaser, not a diver.
- **Holder:** the **elevated shotgun holder**, tip up (a bar wants the highest
  holder so the bar planes on the surface).
- **Top-shot / leader:** **80–100 lb top-shot max**, drag set **well under the
  100 lb internal line** so a hooked fish never loads the bar's internal
  rigging to failure.
- **Newer-model internal line spec (Half Fish):** **100 lb stinger line down the
  middle, 80 lb sides, all Izorline XXX copolymer mono** (not fluoro).

## How to run it

Bars are the light-to-moderate, calm-ish, school-grade play — they run at
whatever the rest of the spread runs (4–14 kt) and go in the elevated shotgun
holder so the bar planes on top. Set the reel drag well under the 100 lb
internal line. **When** to choose a bar over a kite, a speed-trolled
[Mad Mac](../lures/mad-mac.md), or a diver is decided in
[bluefin trolling](../species/bluefin-trolling.md); general spread geometry and
holder elevation are in [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md).
