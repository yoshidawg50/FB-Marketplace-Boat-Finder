# Cedar Plug

The cedar plug is a plain weighted-nose wood cylinder — no bib, no rattle — that
has caught tuna in SoCal/Baja for generations. It's a surface/wash lure that runs
either right in the prop wash or a long way back, and it fishes a
species-matched **40–60 lb-and-up class** depending on rig. Covered here are
standard 6" cedar plugs.

## Running specs

- **Troll speed:** 5–9 kt (**bluefin 5–5.5, yellowfin 6–6.5**).
- **Running depth:** surface / wash.
- **Setback:** either **15–40 ft in the prop wash OR 50–100+ yd back — nothing in
  between** (the two positions where a cedar plug swims right; the dead zone
  between them does not produce).
- **Leader / top-shot:** rig-dependent (see owned example below).

## How to run it

Match the speed to the species (5–5.5 kt for bluefin, 6–6.5 kt for yellowfin) and
place it in one of its two working positions — tight in the wash (15–40 ft) or
way back (50–100+ yd), never the middle. It's the calm, mixed-grade, over-bait
change-up. **When** to reach for a cedar plug versus a skirt, a swimmer, or a
speed-trolled [Mad Mac](../lures/mad-mac.md) is decided in
[bluefin trolling](../species/bluefin-trolling.md); general towing mechanics live
in [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md).

## Owned examples

Profiles link the subset an angler owns. Cameron's **Ahi USA Live Deception 6"
cedar plug** ships factory-rigged with **150 lb fluoro, a 250 lb crane swivel,
and a 10/0 Mustad** — a concrete owned rig for this class. His second plug is
shop-rigged (mono wrapped on the body, swivel, single hook). Both, with rod/
holder pairing, are in
[Cameron's trolling lures](../profiles/cameron/trolling-lures.md).
