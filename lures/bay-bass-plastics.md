# Bay Bass Plastics

The small **finesse soft-plastics** class for the back bays: little scented
sticks, flukes, and grubs fished slow and low for [spotted bay bass
(spotties)](../species/spotted-bay-bass.md) and the mixed bag that shares their
bottom. The bay fish are pressured and not giants, so the whole class runs
**small, light, and bottom-oriented** — a subtle profile that fish will commit to
when they refuse a full-size swimbait. This note owns the baits, heads, and
sizing; how they're fished is [ned rig](../techniques/ned-rig.md) and
[drop shot](../techniques/drop-shot.md).

## The class / models

- **Ned / TRD on a mushroom head** — the core finesse rig. A **mushroom-style Ned
  head** (small lead head) and a **short soft stickbait** — a **ZMan finesse
  TRD** is the benchmark because the elastic plastic survives (**catch 50 fish on
  one bait**). The TRD is tailless, so it's a **bottom-hop** bait, not a straight
  swim. It has a top and bottom (thinner toward the top, tapering thicker at the
  bottom so it sinks better); seat it off the thicker bottom with the hook exiting
  the marked top. Fished [ned rig](../techniques/ned-rig.md).
- **Zoom fluke on the pilings** — a soft jerk/fluke bait worked around **dock
  pilings and structure** where spotties hold; a subtle darting profile fished on
  a light head or weightless/weighted to fall along the piling. Threaded through
  the hook slot like any plastic.
- **Gulp grubs** — scented curly-tail grubs; the **scent is the activator** for
  bottom-feeding bay fish. A **grub swims on a constant retrieve** (rod tip down
  at ~45°, steady wind) on a drop-shot or a light jighead, unlike the tailless
  TRD.
- **Drop-shot plastics** — a **4-inch scented finesse fluke** (ZMan) is the
  standard drop-shot bait; swap it for a **grub or a small swimbait**. Rigged on a
  long-shank J-hook tied so the **hook points up**, suspended **6 to 10 inches**
  above the weight. Fished [drop shot](../techniques/drop-shot.md).
- **Sizes:** small — **4-inch** flukes/grubs and short TRD-size sticks; the fish
  aren't big and the finesse profile is the point.
- **Colors:** natural bay-forage patterns (green pumpkin, morning dawn, motor oil,
  smelt/sardine, root beer). Match the light and the water; keep it subtle.

## How to fish it

- **Ned rig** — [ned rig](../techniques/ned-rig.md): let the mushroom-head TRD
  sink to the bottom watching the line, bounce-and-drop, and **read the bite in
  the line** before you feel it. Light spinning: 10 lb braid, 8 lb leader, a
  4-to-10 lb rod.
- **Drop shot** — [drop shot](../techniques/drop-shot.md): weight on the bottom,
  bait suspended just above, shake it in place; twitch-reel-twitch a fluke, steady
  wind a grub. Light spinning, 10–12 lb leader, light drag.

Router: [spotted bay bass (spotties)](../species/spotted-bay-bass.md).
