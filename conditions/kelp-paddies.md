# Kelp Paddies

Drifting kelp paddies are floating structure — they hold bait, shade, and
gamefish (yellowtail, dorado, paddy bluefin, tripletail, bycatch) out over open
water. The two things worth planning around are **paddy geometry** (how paddies
line up, so you can search efficiently) and **paddy freshness** (which paddies
actually hold fish, so you don't waste the day).

## Paddy geometry — search the line, not the compass

Paddies are not scattered at random; the current that moves them lines them up
(Landesfeind, 7/14/22):

- **Open water:** under the summer uphill current, paddies align roughly
  **north–south**. Find one → search its **N–S line** for the next.
- **Near a bank edge:** paddies align **along the contour**. Follow the contour,
  not the compass.

So the moment you find a productive paddy, you know which direction its neighbors
lie in. Plan a **paddy line**, a route that walks paddy to paddy, rather than a
single stakeout.

**Boats already parked on a paddy** → don't crash it. Look **up and down its
line** for the un-pressured paddies feeding the same drift (8/11/22). The fish on
a worked paddy are the least willing; the ones a quarter mile up the line have
not seen a bait.

## Paddy pressure gradient (SoCal geography)

Paddies are not equal across the Bight (8/17/22):

- The closer to a dock and the more boat traffic, the **less willing** the fish.
- Paddies are **scarcer and hold less** north of Dana Point.
- The **best paddy water is south** — south of Santa Catalina / San Clemente
  Island, and from **Dana Point to Oceanside southward**.

Weight your paddy program toward that southern water when you have the range.

## Paddy freshness — check fast, then move

Fish move paddy-to-paddy **only at night**; during the day the population on a
given paddy is fixed. Therefore **a dry paddy stays dry all day** ("5 Tips"
short). Do not sit on a barren paddy hoping it turns on — it won't until dark.

The discipline:

1. **Check fast** — a look, a pass with the sounder, one bait. Seconds to a
   minute, not twenty.
2. If nothing, **move to the next paddy on the line.**
3. Paddies **restock overnight**, so a paddy that was dry yesterday can be loaded
   this morning. Moon-bright nights drive **bigger redistribution** — expect more
   reshuffling after a bright night. (Paddy timing ties into the
   [moon layer](moon.md).)

This is why the paddy *line* matters more than any single paddy: you are playing
a numbers game across many quick checks.

## Finding paddies

- Glass **down-swell** on gray-calm mornings and watch paddies roll over the
  swell crests; general method in
  [search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).
- **Birds** mark paddies. **Cameron's model** (source `cameron`): **gulls are
  paddy-finders** — "good sign but dumb," a general measure of life, and reliable
  for locating floating kelp even when they aren't marking gamefish. See
  [bird reading](bird-reading.md) for the full bird model, corpus + Cameron
  side by side.
