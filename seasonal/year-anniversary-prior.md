# Year-Anniversary Prior

**This is a prior — a pattern-layer heuristic, not current intel.** It tells you
where to *start looking*, to be refined the moment you pull live
[BightSST](../planning/day-plan-protocol.md) conditions.

## The prior — the bluefin route repeats year over year

**"Go back to this date last year and the year before — pretty close to where
they'll be"** (10/12/22). The [bluefin](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) migration route
repeats year over year, so the same calendar week in prior years is a usable
prior for where the fish are now.

- **Strongest in fall.** The repeatability is most reliable in the fall run —
  weight the anniversary prior most heavily then.
- **Duane's version — project the travel line.** Line up the prior days' catch
  positions and **project the travel line** forward (Capt. Duane Diego Mellor).
  Rather than a single anniversary point, use the recent trend of positions to
  extrapolate where the fish are heading.
- Pair the prior with the advection model — open-water bluefin move
  **10–15 nm overnight** — when you project forward. See
  [report reading and forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md).

This is a **same-week historical prior**, not a promise. After a full moon or
late in the season, when the fleet loses the fish, widen the search prior toward
the same-date prior-year locations rather than trusting report persistence.

## Conflict, kept side by side — Cameron's water-state model

**Cameron holds the opposite framing for [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md)** and it
is kept here attributed, not reconciled:

**Observed** (Cameron, stated 7/27/26): for yellowtail the **calendar date /
month doesn't matter** — the fish follow **water temperature and bait**, and the
bait follows temperature and currents. The calendar patterns in his catch data
are **downstream of water state**, not a driver of it (source: cameron).

### The tension, stated explicitly

- The corpus **year-anniversary prior** says the route recurs on the *calendar*
  — go to last year's date.
- Cameron's model says the *calendar is an artifact*; what recurs is the **water
  state**, and the fish track that. If the water is early or late this year, the
  fish are early or late with it.

These are not merged. Note the two framings apply to different fish and
different evidence: the anniversary prior is a **bluefin-route** observation from
the corpus; Cameron's is a **yellowtail** model from his own catch data.
Treat the anniversary prior as a *starting box* and always let live water state
— [SST](../conditions/water-temperature.md), [chlorophyll](../conditions/water-color.md),
[current](../conditions/current-structure.md), and
[upwelling / turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md) — override it. Cameron's
open framing is attributed doctrine, not a gate.
