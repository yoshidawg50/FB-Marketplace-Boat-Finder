# Tuna Feather Rig

Rigging a tuna feather — a West Coast trolling staple. The skirt slides over a
chrome or lead head; a barrel swivel joins the leader to the hook and lets the
hook swing and reset. Feathers come in a range of sizes, shapes, and colors, from
a heavier jet head (holes drilled so it smokes, swims across varied sea
conditions) down to small mini-marlin-lure style heads used when fish are on
small bait. It is a great all-around lure for most everything that swims offshore.

## When to use it

- A general-purpose bait in the [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md) spread. See
  [tuna feathers and skirts](../lures/tuna-feathers-and-skirts.md) for the lure
  itself and where each size/color fits the spread.

## Parameters & judgment

- **Thread the skirt on first:** flip the lure onto the leader before tying
  anything — a common miss that forces a re-tie if skipped.
- **Leader:** about a 6-foot section of leader material, 60 to 80 pound test.
  Lighter 40 pound was the old standard but tends to chafe unnoticed and snap off;
  60 to 80 is fine for tuna unless the fish are very picky.
- **Knots:** a barrel swivel is tied to each end of the leader with whatever knot
  you are confident in — improved clinch, San Diego, or uni all work.
- **Hook:** a tuna hook, about a size 9/0 in the demo. Feed the hook point through
  the eye of the barrel swivel; match hook and swivel ahead of time so the point
  will pass through the round end of the swivel. Secure the hook to the swivel
  with a zip tie (a smaller zip tie is better so it does not affect the action),
  then trim it.
- **Swivel does double duty:** it lets the hook swing in any direction and spin
  back into position if the lure gets knocked or fouled, and it spaces the hook
  off the head.
- **Hook position:** slide the lure down onto the swivel so the bend of the hook
  sits about even with the end of the skirt — it can ride up inside an inch or so,
  but keep it close to the back. Too far forward and the skirt fouls the hook
  points; kept back in the lighter skirt material, the point comes through cleanly
  and finds the jaw.
