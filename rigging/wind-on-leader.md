# Wind-On Leader

A wind-on leader is a fluorocarbon (or mono) top shot joined to hollow-core
braid without a knot: the leader is threaded *inside* the hollow braid so the
braid's weave clamps down on it (the Chinese-finger-trap principle), and the
braid is finished with its own doubled loop on the reel end. The result is a
near-100%-strength connection with almost no diameter bump — smooth enough to
wind through the rod guides and back onto the reel. It swaps in and out at the
loop, so a fresh top shot goes on in seconds without re-spooling. See
[line and leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md) for the braid and fluoro classes
this pairs with.

## When to use it

- Any application where the leader must pass through the guides during the fight
  and you want no knot catching on the way out: tuna [fly-lining](../techniques/flyline.md),
  [knife jigging](../techniques/knife-jigging.md), [kite fishing](../techniques/kite-fishing.md).
- Fast top-shot changes on the water — carry pre-made wind-ons of different
  lengths/classes and connect them loop-to-loop instead of retying.
- Requires **hollow-core** braid (e.g. 16-strand thread-lock type) on the reel;
  it will not work with solid braid.

## Parameters & judgment

- **Match the classes.** Keep braid and leader within roughly one pound-test
  step of each other — 100 lb braid to 100 lb fluoro works, and 130 lb braid to
  100 lb leader is acceptable; a large mismatch such as 130 lb braid to 40 lb
  leader will not hold. Pick the leader diameter closest to the braid's rating.
- **Bury length is the whole game.** The longer the fluoro sits inside the braid,
  the harder the finger-trap grips. Treat roughly **2 ft as the minimum** for the
  leader-into-braid splice; do not be stingy — cut a long working section (about
  6 ft) so you have slack to feed in. Older doctrine ran the bury around
  five to six feet with a served finish; both hold, the shorter version leans
  harder on a locked terminal wrap.
- **The braid loop** on the reel end is a doubled, tucked loop sized to pass your
  wrist through (for tying a bimini/cat's-paw to the running braid). Adjust it
  before cutting anything off the spool.
- **Lock the tail.** The bare finger-trap will slide out under slack, so finish
  the leader end — either a served wrap of dental floss / 30–65 lb braid closed
  with a three-turn uni, or a series of ~15–20 same-direction half hitches
  finished with a full hitch that tucks the tag two strands back. Set it with a
  drop of super glue.
- **Leader length** is builder's choice — common finished top shots run about
  25 ft. Prep several (e.g. 100 lb, various lengths) and store them coiled.
- **Failure modes:** peeling braid off over the top (adds a half-twist that jams
  the needle — strip it straight instead); pushing the needle through the wall of
  the braid at too steep an angle; too short a bury; skipping the terminal
  lock so the leader creeps out on slack.
