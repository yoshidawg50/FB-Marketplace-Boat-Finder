# Swordfish (Broadbill)

SoCal **daytime deep-drop** swordfish: find deep contour structure where the
[deep scattering layer](../conditions/deep-scattering-layer.md) holds bait, then
present a dead bait in/below the layer and let the fish come to you. This note
**routes**; the method is [deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md).
All parameters here are **SoCal Bight**. Primary source: the Costa deep-drop
seminar (Feb 2021 — Duane Diego Mellor, Rush Maltz, Ali Hussainy; the seasons it
describes are **2019 and 2020**).

## Where & when

- **Season:** late **September – December**; peak **October – mid-November**
  (interannual — some years hot late Sept/Oct, others mid-Nov; last fish ~a week
  before Christmas). Holdover fish are around year-round but generally **not
  worth the fuel Jan–Aug**. See [Nov–Dec priors](../seasonal/november-december.md).
- **Where:** **150–350 fathoms** on contour lines, thumbs, notches, humps.
  Named working spots: the Ridge, the 9, the 43, the 178, the 302, the 138/182
  area on break days. West-coast model — **set up and let the fish come along
  the fathom-curve highways** (see [island structure](../locations/island-structure.md),
  [zone lexicon](../locations/zone-lexicon.md)).
- **Fishable day:** a fair-weather fishery — **>15 kt = no-go**, comfortable
  **10–13 kt**, drift **≤ ~2–2.5 kt** (a drift sock extends marginal days). See
  [sea-state](../conditions/sea-state.md); drift mechanics in the
  [technique note](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md).
- **Moon:** bites rise on **new and full and the flanking weeks**
  ([moon](../conditions/moon.md)).
- **Water:** SST + chlorophyll breaks = converging masses; prefer **one big
  powerful "highway" break** over finger rips. **Structure × current-edge
  intersection = the banger days** ("you could have been there a week earlier and
  couldn't buy a bite"). See [water color](../conditions/water-color.md),
  [water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md).

## Finding them (sign & sonar)

The fish **ride the DSL by day** (sat-tag work, PIER / Sepúlveda), so you hunt
the *layer and its bait*, not the fish. Requirements before you stop:

- **DSL present at ≥ 600 ft** — prefer the **900 ft – 150 fathom** band.
- **DSL *plus* chunky bait balls** (anchovy / hake / squid) at **100–150+
  fathoms ≈ bites near-assured.** A bare layer with no bait is not a stop.
- **Sounder for the DSL:** TVG / gain pushed up (**Furuno ~55–57**; mid-high on
  other brands), ping speed slowed (**~15 of 20**) to paint the layer. On
  Cameron's **Garmin GPSMAP 840xs**: gain up, slower ping, watch for the band.
  Full method: [electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).
- **Diet corroboration:** stomachs full of hake, small rockfish, squid
  (bottom-adjacent feeders); one Catalina fish full of mullet (they range
  inshore at night).

## Situations → techniques

| Situation (conditions) | Do this | Gear class | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deep contour break (150–350 fm), DSL ≥600 ft **+** bait balls at 100–150 fm, wind <13 kt, drift ≤2–2.5 kt | **[Daytime deep-drop](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)** — bait in/below the DSL, drift up-current of the fathom line | Heavy stand-up 2-speed lever-drag class + heavy bite-leader class | Banger-day setup |
| DSL present but **no** bait balls / no rip | Keep moving — grade the meter, don't drop on a bare layer | — | Bait *in* the DSL is the trigger |
| Marginal wind (13–15 kt) or drift >2.5 kt | [Deep-drop](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md) with a **drift sock**; if still >15 kt, **no-go** | + drift sock | Presentation dies with too much drift |
| Bite window | Weight toward **new/full moon ± flanking weeks** and the biggest highway break | — | Moon + structure×edge stack the odds |

## Gear summary (class terms)

- **Rod/reel:** heavy stand-up, **2-speed lever-drag class**. The fishery uses
  **both electric-assist and manual (non-electric) reels** — pick per program.
- **Terminal:** heavy **bite-leader class** (200 lb+), a breakaway **7–10.5 lb**
  lead matched to the drift, short leader. Rig parameters live in the
  [technique note](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md) and
  [bite leaders](../rigging/bite-leaders.md). Class terms: [gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md).
- **Bait:** dead baits (squid, mackerel, mullet-class) fished deep.

## Doctrine & conflicts

- **Doctrine (high — Duane Diego Mellor / Rush Maltz / Ali Hussainy, 2021
  seminar):** the DSL + bait-ball rule and the structure×current-edge "banger
  day" model are the core.
- **Region:** these are **SoCal Bight** parameters. East-coast/Gulf Stream
  deep-dropping is bottom-oriented (~1,800 ft), heavier leads, long leaders — a
  different game; the labeled contrast is in the
  [technique note](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md).
- **Cameron (profile constraint, not fishery doctrine):** his program would be
  **manual reels only** with a library of banked bulk braid spools — that is his
  choice, recorded in [his rods](../profiles/cameron/rods.md), not a rule of the
  fishery.
