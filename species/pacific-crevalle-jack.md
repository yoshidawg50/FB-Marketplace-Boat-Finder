# Pacific Crevalle Jack (Toro)

The **Pacific crevalle jack** (*Caranx caninus*, "toro") is a **warm-water,
inshore structure gamefish** — common in Baja (Sea of Cortez, BOLA, farther
south) and a **rare warm-water visitor to SoCal** in strong warm-water years.
It fishes much like a yellowtail: a hard-pulling, structure-oriented jack that
eats irons, poppers, and bait around points, boils, and rocky shorelines.
**This species is thin in the source corpus** — no dated report or academy
episode covers it — so this note is a short, correct router built from general
SoCal/Baja knowledge plus Cameron's stated interest, at **confidence: low**
(see **Doctrine & conflicts**).

## Where & when

- **Baja (primary range):** warm months, inshore — around **rocky points,
  structure, boils, and river/estuary mouths** in the Sea of Cortez, around
  **[BOLA](../seasonal/september.md)**, and south. Most reliable when the water
  is warm. See [water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md).
- **SoCal (rare):** a **warm-water stray** — expect them only when a strong
  warm-water push (warm-year / El Niño-type conditions) carries tropical fish
  north into range. Peak warm-water window is late summer into fall
  ([August](../seasonal/august.md), [September](../seasonal/september.md)).

## Finding them (sign & sonar)

- **Visual sign:** **surface boils and bait busting near inshore structure** —
  points, rock piles, boils. Working birds over the boil help locate them; see
  [bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md) and
  [search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).
- **Sonar:** treat like a yellowtail-class structure fish — **marks near
  points/structure in the mid-to-upper column** over hard bottom. *No
  species-specific sonar signature is documented in this corpus* — the depth read
  below is inferred from the general structure-jack pattern, not from a source.
  General method: [electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).

## Gear summary (class terms)

- **Rod/reel:** jig-stick class for casting, **40–60 lb class** for yo-yo and
  bait — a hard-fighting jack that rewards backbone. See
  [gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md).
- **Lures:** [iron jigs](../lures/iron-jigs.md) and
  [poppers/stickbaits](../lures/tuna-poppers-and-stickbaits.md).
- **Terminal:** strong, abrasion-tolerant leader — they fight brutally around
  structure and are not leader-shy, so heavier terminal costs few bites.
