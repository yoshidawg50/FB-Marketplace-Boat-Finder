# California Barracuda

A **nearshore pelagic** that patrols the kelp lines, boiler zones, and beach
edges and shows up in the same water as the coastal bass. In the SoCal corpus
barracuda are almost always a **mixed-bag component** — one of the "three B's"
(bass, barracuda, bonito) you pick up while fishing the kelp and structure
([calico bass](calico-bass.md), [bonito](bonito.md)). They have real **teeth**,
so the terminal end changes when you target them. Confidence is medium: the
corpus records them as frequent bycatch in the dated reports rather than as a
dedicated program.

## Where & when

- **Where:** the **kelp lines, boiler rocks, and beach edges** along the coast
  and around the islands — the same structure edges the bass relate to. Reported
  "along the beach you're getting barracuda, calicos, and sand bass," up in
  Santa Monica Bay, and around the Channel Islands (Santa Cruz / Anacapa)
  ([bass structure](../locations/bass-structure.md),
  [break walls, jetties and riprap](../locations/breakwalls-jetties-riprap.md)).
- **When:** a warm-season nearshore fishery. Barracuda appear in the reports from
  **late spring into fall** — mixed into the bass and bonito counts from May
  ([May](../seasonal/may.md)) through the summer
  ([June–July](../seasonal/june-july.md), [August](../seasonal/august.md)). The
  "three B's" show together at the Channel Islands on a variety day.

## Finding them (sign & sonar)

- **Sign:** barracuda run in **loose schools along the kelp line and at anchor
  over structure** — "nice bass, barracuda when they're anchored up." They mix
  into surface activity with bonito, so **boils, breezing fish, and working
  birds** over a kelp edge can hold a mix of the two ([bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md)).
- **Sonar signature:** they mark as **scattered, higher-in-the-column fish
  suspended along the kelp/structure edge** rather than pinned to the bottom like
  the bass — a looser, more mobile sign in the same zone. Grade the bait picture
  the same way you would for bass ([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md),
  [search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)).

## Situations → techniques

| Situation (conditions) | Do this | Gear class | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fish breezing/boiling along the kelp line | **Surface iron** ([surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)) — cast and **retrieve fast**; barracuda chase a moving target | jig-stick class, [iron jigs](../lures/iron-jigs.md), [gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md) | A fast, flashy retrieve triggers the chase; small chrome jigs work when they're on small bait |
| Barracuda mixed with bass on a structure edge, live bait aboard | **Flyline** a sardine/mackerel ([flyline](../techniques/flyline.md)) | flyline class, 25–40 lb | The same drift that produces calico turns up barracuda |
| Getting bit off / cut leaders | Add a **short single-strand wire** ([haywire twist](../rigging/haywire-twist.md)) or step up to heavy hard mono | wire / heavy-mono terminal | Barracuda teeth slice mono, even heavy; wire ends the cut-offs but can cost bites — heavy hard mono is the line-shy compromise |
| Fast, flashy chase bite | **Small casting jigs / fast retrieves** | light casting class | Keep it moving; a dead-stopped bait gets ignored |

## Gear summary (class terms)

- **Rod/reel:** the coastal **inshore bass / jig-stick classes** cover barracuda —
  a jig-stick for surface iron, a flyline setup for live bait. See
  [gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md).
- **Terminal:** the change from bass gear is the **bite tippet** — a short
  single-strand wire tied with a [haywire twist](../rigging/haywire-twist.md), or
  heavier hard mono, to survive the teeth. Surface [iron jigs](../lures/iron-jigs.md)
  and small casting jigs.

## Doctrine & conflicts

- **Doctrine (medium):** treat barracuda as a **mixed-bag opportunist** on the
  kelp lines and boiler zones — fish them on the fast surface-iron retrieve or a
  flylined bait, and rig for the teeth when you start getting cut off. The corpus
  does not carry a dedicated barracuda technique program; everything here is
  inferred from repeated report mentions of them alongside bass and bonito, so it
  is held at medium confidence pending a dedicated source.
