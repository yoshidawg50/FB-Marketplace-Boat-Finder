# Cameron's Boat — Panga Marine Marquesas 22

Operating envelope and trolling platform for Cameron's boat. The day-plan
protocol reads this to keep recommendations inside the boat's real limits
(range, sea-state, holder geometry). Universal trolling *doctrine* Cameron
stated (rod-tip elevation rule, the 4-factor rod↔lure framework) lives in
[trolling](../../techniques/trolling.md); this note holds the platform facts.

## Hull & power

- **2004 Panga Marine Marquesas 22**, single **115 hp Yamaha** outboard.
- Tops out **~35 kt**; idles **~1.5 kt**.
- Foam-filled hull, **very small bilge**. Slight hull flex / "spring" on hard
  wave impacts, no cracking (noted July 2026).
- **No trim tabs** — engine trim is the only attitude control.
- **No AC / shore-power inlet, no house battery** — bait tank and accessories
  run off the **single starting battery**: Interstate/Costco Group 24DC
  deep-cycle (**550 CCA, 700 MCA, 140 min RC, 41 lb**; ~2 yrs old as of July
  2026). **NOCO GEN5X1** onboard marine charger (12V/5A, hardwired AC pigtail)
  added July 2026 to hold the battery up while the bait pump runs overnight.
- Jackery policy: Explorer 240 can live in a dry compartment while fishing; the
  1500 Plus never leaves the bay (too expensive/heavy).

## Bait & ice

- **Bait tank: 30 gallons.** Pump is a **Rule 401 Tournament Series, 500 GPH,
  12V, 2.1 A draw, 3 A fuse**. Bilge pump is **manual-switch only, no float
  switch**. Tank is dialed — bait normally survives well (see
  [bait tanks](../../bait/bait-tanks.md)).
- **Ice: 100–125 lb per trip.**

## Rod holders & trolling geometry

- **10 rod holders total; 6 vertical.**
- **Trolling set:** two gunnel holders per side at **15°** + one elevated
  **shotgun behind the seat at 5°** (shotgun sits **~3 ft higher and ~4 ft
  further forward** than the gunnels; tip **~4 ft above the water**, pointed
  straight up).
- Owns **rod-holder outrigger extensions** (angled extenders that drop into the
  gunwale holders) and trolls rods from them; exploring extra-long (~10 ft)
  heavy rods in the extenders to widen the spread without installing outriggers.
- **No outriggers.** Normally runs **2–3 lures**; the **furthest-back lure runs
  out of the elevated shotgun** holder.
- **Divers want the gunnel holders; bars and surface skirts want the elevated
  shotgun** (rod-tip elevation trades running depth — see
  [trolling](../../techniques/trolling.md)).
- Chose **spreader bars** over conventional bars — no room on the panga for
  conventional bars (see [spreader bar](../../lures/spreader-bar.md)).

## Trim / ballast routine

- Runs with **at least one person forward** when underway; big bean bag on the
  bow for the rider (rough days forward are brutal — rider catches air then hits
  the deck).
- **Running out:** ~100 lb ice in the cooler, one person on the bow bean bag +
  one on the seat forward of the console.
- **Coming in:** drains the 30-gal bait tank (heavy stern water) and shifts one
  person aft to offset.
- **Air draft:** tall VHF antenna, but antenna and all rods lay flat — leaving
  his head / helm seat as the highest point.

## Ops norms

- Normal departure **~8 am** (earlier starts are the exception).
- Launches from **Dana Landing (Mission Bay)** (e.g. the 8/5/26 trip).
- Trailer is very back-heavy (low tongue weight) — minimizes highway towing; for
  Catalina he weighs a shorter tow vs a longer crossing.
- **Home waters:** Coronado Islands, La Jolla, San Diego offshore banks; regular
  Baja trips, especially Bahía de los Ángeles (BOLA).
- **Data:** chartplotter is a **Garmin GPSMAP 840xs** (sounder settings in
  [electronics & sounder](../../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

## Which setups troll

Commonly trolls the **Talica 25** and **Fathom 80** setups, plus sometimes the
**Trinidad 40**; the **TLD 20** is entering rotation for diver duty. **Never**
trolls the Lexa 400. Full rod inventory and line classes: [rods](rods.md).
Owned trolling lures + running specs: [trolling lures](trolling-lures.md).

## Open items (attributed, not doctrine)

- Exploring extra-long (~10 ft) heavy rods in the gunwale extenders to push the
  spread wider without outriggers.
- Wants a drill-powered retrieve option for winding up between spots instead of
  an expensive electric reel.
