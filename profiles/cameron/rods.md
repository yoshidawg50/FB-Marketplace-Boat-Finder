# Cameron's Rods — 8 Setups

Cameron's rod/reel inventory. The **braid weight listed is the max line he'll put
on that rod**; top shots / leaders change per application. Class terms link to
[gear classes](../../tackle/gear-classes.md); see
[rod & reel selection](../../tackle/rod-and-reel-selection.md) for the general
logic. **Every setup except the Fathom 80 has caught a bluefin.**

## 1. Trinidad 40 setup
- **Rod:** M&M Custom on blank "RA96XH 40-80" (researched ID, high confidence:
  Rainshadow RCJB96XH, **8'0", XH, 40–80 lb, 4–10 oz**).
- **Reel:** Shimano Trinidad 40. **Braid: 80 lb.**
- **Role:** heavy setup — [yo-yo iron](../../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md), sinker
  rigs, heavy [flyline](../../techniques/flyline.md), some trolling; relatively
  soft tip; can cast large surface irons but not well.

## 2. Avet setup
- **Rod:** Shimano Teramar West Coast TMC90MHB2 (**9'0", 2-pc, MH, fast, 30–65 lb
  braid class**).
- **Reel:** Avet MXJ 5.8 MC. **Braid: 50 lb.**
- **Role:** mainly [flyline](../../techniques/flyline.md).

## 3. Tranx setup
- **Rod:** Phenix M1 Inshore SMX-82H (**8'2", 1-pc, H, extra-fast, 20–45 lb,
  3–8 oz**).
- **Reel:** Shimano Tranx 400. **Braid: TBD** (respool pending — no weight
  recorded).
- **Role:** utility — flyline sometimes, lures sometimes.

## 4. Lexa 400 setup
- **Rod:** Okuma PCH Custom PCH-C-901H (**9'0", 1-pc, H, fast, ~20–60 lb**).
- **Reel:** Daiwa Lexa-HD 400HS-P. **Braid: 50 lb.**
- **Role:** his biggest workhorse rod. **Never for trolling.**

## 5. Torium setup
- **Rod:** Phenix Abyss PSX1009J / PSX-C 1009 (**10'0", 25–60 lb, moderate/fast**,
  deckhand cord-wrapped handle, clamp-on reel).
- **Reel:** Shimano Torium 20HG. **Braid: 50 lb.**
- **Role:** full [surface iron](../../techniques/surface-iron.md) setup.

## 6. Talica 25 setup
- **Rod:** Phenix Axis HAX-C 800X3H (**8'0", 3X-H, 60–130 lb, rail-rod class**),
  cross-cut 4-slot aluminum gimbal butt.
- **Reel:** Shimano Talica 25II 2-speed. **Braid: 100 lb.**
- **Role:** main heavy setup, commonly **trolling**; the rod he'd cast at a jumbo
  **(80–140 lb)** bluefin [foamer](../../techniques/foamer-casting.md) school.

## 7. Fathom 80 setup
- **Rod:** Okuma PCH Custom PCH-C-741XXXXH (**7'4", XXXX-H, fast, 150–200 lb**),
  cross-cut gimbal butt.
- **Reel:** Penn Fathom II 80LD2 2-speed (**4.2:1 / 1.6:1, 50 lb max drag**);
  **~1000 yd of 100 lb braid.**
- **Role:** heaviest setup; **[spreader-bar](../../lures/spreader-bar.md)
  trolling only — never casts**. Future [kite](../../techniques/kite-fishing.md)
  rod (keep 100 lb braid for capacity and less wind-blow; 200 lb leader; small
  boat can chase). Considers the 100 lb braid lighter than needed for some uses —
  may strip to a bulk spool and bank it.

## 8. TLD 20 setup
- **Rod:** Seeker 660H (**6'0", H, 30–80 lb, E-glass, American Series class**).
- **Reel:** Shimano TLD 20 single-speed lever drag. **Braid: 80 lb.**
- **Role:** rarely used; entering trolling rotation for **diver duty** (Halcos,
  Husky Magnums) plus skirt / cedar-plug work on school-grade days.

## Open items (attributed, not doctrine)

- **Setup 3 (Tranx)** braid weight pending respool — do not record a weight yet.
- Shopping a **dedicated SPJ / speed-jig setup** for night bluefin +
  September BOLA bottom yellowtail (one setup for both if possible) — see
  [slow-pitch jigging](../../techniques/slow-pitch-jigging.md) and
  [knife jigging](../../techniques/knife-jigging.md).
- May strip the Fathom 80's 100 lb braid to a bulk spool and bank it; wants a
  library of banked bulk braid spools to swap reel setups per program (e.g.
  future daytime swordfish on manual reels).
