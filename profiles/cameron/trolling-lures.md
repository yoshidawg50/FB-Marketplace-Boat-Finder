# Cameron's Trolling Lures (owned)

Cameron's owned trolling spread (inventory complete as of 7/23/26, ~25 lures incl.
the unidentified skirt bag). **Running specs** — troll speeds, running depths,
setbacks, leader/top-shot per model — are the spec backbone of the linked
[lures](../../lures/) notes; this note is the owned subset. How he decides which
to pull is in [bluefin trolling](../../species/bluefin-trolling.md) and
[trolling](../../techniques/trolling.md); boat spread geometry is in
[boat](boat.md).

- **Nomad DTX Minnow 165** — purple/magenta, blue-white-black lateral stripe —
  new in box. → [DTX minnow](../../lures/dtx-minnow.md)
- **Rapala Husky Magnum 25** — blue mackerel — heavily used, rusted trebles.
- **Rapala Husky Magnum 25** (second) — black back / blue bars over gold-olive
  holo, pink head, chartreuse back edge.
- **Rapala Husky Magnum 15** — purple/green holo, black diagonal bars, silver
  belly. → [Rapala Husky Magnum](../../lures/rapala-husky-magnum.md)
- **Halco Laser Pro 190 XDD ×2** (6M+ "crazy deep" bib) — purple/chrome and
  pink. → [Halco Laser Pro](../../lures/halco-laser-pro.md)

## Spreader bars → [spreader bar](../../lures/spreader-bar.md)
- **Three Half Fish Bait Co spreader bars** — purple/black · green/orange ·
  blue/chartreuse/silver. Bought at Fred Hall; the March 2026 one has a slight
  bend = Half Fish's newer model. **Newer-model line spec (per Half Fish):
  100 lb stinger line down the middle, 80 lb sides, all Izorline XXX copolymer
  mono (not fluoro).** First season using bars; went to Half Fish for guidance
  because little is published.

## Spread rules (attributed cameron; universal doctrine → [trolling](../../techniques/trolling.md))
- No outriggers; normally runs **2–3 lures**; furthest-back lure runs out of the
  elevated shotgun holder.
- Can run any top-shot strength — wants a **top-shot recommended per lure** when
  planning a day.
- Trolls on the Talica 25 and Fathom 80 setups plus sometimes the Trinidad 40;
  TLD 20 entering rotation for diver duty. **Never** trolls the Lexa 400.
