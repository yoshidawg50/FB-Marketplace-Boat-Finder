# Cameron's Tackle — Hard Baits, Casting & Jigging (owned)

Cameron's owned casting/jigging hard baits (inventory complete as of 7/23/26).
Universal specs and technique live in the linked class notes; this is the
subset he owns. Class terms: [gear classes](../../tackle/gear-classes.md).

## Surface irons — [iron jigs](../../lures/iron-jigs.md), fished [surface iron](../../techniques/surface-iron.md)
- **Squidco Candy Bar SR Lite** — scrambled egg — 3 oz / 6.5"; moderate steady
  grind, optional sink count.
- **Strictly Irons Glory** — light blue — 7" CNC surface iron, big
  cruising-mackerel profile; wide drawn-out kick, slow and steady; change-up for
  picky bites / slack current.
- **Strictly Irons Mighty** — tan — 5.5" CNC surface iron; medium-to-high-speed
  retrieve.
- **JRI-5** — scrambled egg — biggest JRI surface iron; slow-to-medium on heavy
  line; BOLA-proven.
- **JRI-7** — light blue — ~Tady 45 class; medium-fast; local / kelp all-rounder.
- **Nomad Slidekick 120g** — Spanish mackerel — 6" PVC-over-steel surface iron,
  4.25 oz; random side-kick; heavier than traditional irons, can be sunk out to
  cover the column.

## Yo-yo irons — [iron jigs](../../lures/iron-jigs.md), fished [yo-yo iron](../../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md)
- **Salas 6X** — blue — classic heavy yo-yo iron; drop to marks, full-speed crank.
- **Salas CP 105** — silver/chrome — heavy yo-yo-class iron.

## Casting jigs / minnows
- **Daiwa Zakana 100g** — tan — casting jig 3.5 oz; subtle side-to-side on fast
  retrieve, stays horizontal; cast / jig / troll.
- **Daiwa SP Minnow 15F** — tan — 6" floating minnow (surf / skinny water).
  **SP Minnow 15S** — tan — sinking.
- **Yo-Zuri Hydro Minnow "LC 205"** — green/white/orange — size unverified
  (documented LC line is 150/170 mm only; **Cameron to verify** — open item).
- **Jansen Cabo Killer** — white/blue — sinking ~2 oz; fast straight retrieve,
  tip up; Baja surf staple.

## Tuna poppers / stickbaits — [tuna poppers & stickbaits](../../lures/tuna-poppers-and-stickbaits.md)
- **Clear Choice Ghost Popper 150** — clear — 150 mm / 2.1 oz wire-through
  popper; crimp 100 lb+ or split ring; SoCal bluefin standard.
- **Clear Choice Ghost TP 140** and **TP 160** — clear — tail-prop ploppers;
  straight cast-and-retrieve (the TP slow-troll claim is marketing only — no
  independent trolling reports found, July 2026).

## Experimental (open items — attributed, not doctrine)
