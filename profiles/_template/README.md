# Profile Template

Copy this folder to `profiles/<your-name>/`, fill in the five files, then point
the [day-plan protocol](../../planning/day-plan-protocol.md) (or the
[boat-day skill](../../skills/boat-day/README.md)) at your profile. The KB works
with **no** profile — it recommends in class terms — and **sharpens** as you add
detail. Everything you enter links out to the universal notes.

## What you provide

| File | What goes in it | Why it sharpens the plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [boat.md](boat.md) | Operating envelope: hull, power, top speed, range, sea-state comfort, bait tank, ice, **rod-holder count + angles/geometry**, home port | Bounds the plan to reachable zones, fishable sea states, and a spread your holders can actually fly |
| [rods.md](rods.md) | Each rod/reel setup with its **line class** and role | Lets the plan resolve a [gear class](../../tackle/gear-classes.md) to the exact rod you own |
| [tackle.md](tackle.md) | Owned casting/jigging hard baits (irons, jigs, poppers) | Recommends from your box, not a generic list |
| [trolling-lures.md](trolling-lures.md) | Owned trolling spread + any known running specs | Builds a spread from what you carry |
| [spots.md](spots.md) | Home waters, home port/range, optional named spots (+ coords) | Anchors zone selection; **withhold coordinates on a public repo unless you've decided they're not sensitive** |

## How recommendations degrade / sharpen

- **No profile** → "reach for the surface-iron class, 40–60 lb" (class terms).
- **With a profile** → "your Torium setup, 50 lb braid" — and the plan respects
  your boat's range, sea-state limit, and holder geometry.

Provenance for your own entries: use `sources: [<your-handle>]` in front matter
(add your handle to [`sources/source-registry.md`](../../sources/source-registry.md)
if you want your doctrine to count as high confidence), and keep conflicts with
the corpus side by side, attributed — never silently reconciled.
