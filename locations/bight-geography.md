# Bight Geography

The big-picture map of the Southern California Bight for planning: which zones the
NW wind wrecks and which ride it out, the path the warm water band tracks through
fall, and where kelp-paddy water actually lives. This is universal geography — no
coordinates. It sits above the point-by-point structure in
[island structure](island-structure.md) and feeds
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md).

## Wind-rollover geography

A big NW blow does not hit the whole bight equally. Its geography is repeatable
(4/21/22, Landesfeind):

- **Gets crushed:** the **northern Channel Islands** and the **LA / Ventura
  coastal shelf** — a strong NW event can roll the surface temperature down
  **5–8 °F** there.
- **Less prone:** **south of Newport** is "less prone to wind-wave-driven
  rollovers" — the geometry shelters it from the same NW fetch.
- **Held through it:** the **Coronados, the tuna grounds, Catalina, and San
  Clemente Island** held their water through the blow in that event.

Planning use: when a NW event is forecast, weight zones by this map — expect the
northern islands and LA/Ventura shelf to have cooled and turned over, and look
south (Newport-and-below, Coronados, SCI, Catalina) for water that survived. The
mechanism (upwelling, the cold-green rollover) is in
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md); the sea-state
consequences in [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md).

## The warm-band tracking path (fall)

In fall the regime flips to "look 100% at your temp chart and chlorophyll," and
the warm water moves along a **repeatable path** (10/12–10/20/22 reports):

**backside Santa Cruz / Rosa → Santa Barbara Island → backside Catalina → hooks
to the 499 / Clemente.**

As the coast **cools and fragments**, the bluefin **condense outward toward
Tanner and Cortez**. So the fall search prior is not a fixed spot but a *band in
motion* — follow the warm, clean edge along that arc, and expect the terminal
condensation onto the outer banks as the season closes. Temperature and
chlorophyll weighting for this regime lives in
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md) and
[water color](../conditions/water-color.md).

## Paddy-water geography

Kelp paddies are not evenly distributed across the bight (8/17/22):

- **Best paddy water:** **south of San Clemente Island / Catalina**, and along
  the coastal strip from **Dana Point to Oceanside and southward**.
- **Scarcer and less holding:** **north of Dana Point** — paddies thin out and
  hold fewer fish up there.

Combined with the paddy pressure gradient (the closer to a dock and the more boat
traffic, the less willing the fish), this tells you where to spend paddy-hunting
time. Paddy mechanics — how they restock at night, how to read one — are in
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md). The species most tied to this
water is [bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) (paddy yellows and yellowfin
share it).
